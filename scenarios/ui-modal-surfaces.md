**U3512c the shared surface has soft depth and real buttons.** Given each U3512b surface in Dark and Light at 1512×950, then it is opaque, rounded, bounded by one hairline border and uses at most one tight shadow; a modal backdrop dims the window with no blur halo. Policy/critic: surface radius, border, shadow, scrim and button fills/radii are Tokens with Light and Dark values documented in `docs/design-system.md`. The modal/panel shadow has zero horizontal offset, vertical offset at most 4 px, blur at most 12 px, no positive spread and alpha at most 0.24; no stacked outer shadows or backdrop blur. A shadow is optional; a border is required. Native alerts retain system-owned appearance. Preserve the Terminal Bubble's existing background-Agent focus treatment; do not add blur to the modal backdrop.

Policy/critic: modal actions and dialog/panel action footers use pill-shaped buttons with visible filled surfaces: the primary uses the accent with a contrasting label, the secondary uses a neutral grey fill. Other modal action controls, including recovery actions and Close, are real buttons with a visible surface, never bare text links. Menu choices remain menu items and editable fields retain their input semantics. Buttons meet the existing type/space steps, 24 px minimum target height, contrast and keyboard focus Checks. Hover changes ink without adding a new box or moving any element. The critic checks every audited surface in both schemes, including long Unicode names, ten items, ownership/consequence warnings and visible failures; visual appearance is not asserted in unit tests.

Given a submittable form, when Enter activates its primary action, then exactly one submit occurs; Escape activates its secondary dismissal without submitting. A multiline field keeps its existing newline chord and explicit submit chord; Enter selecting a picker option never also submits its parent. Help/recovery surfaces with no submit have Close as their dismissal, not an invented primary mutation. Disabled/pending/invalid primary actions never submit. Tests: `u3512c_form_buttons_keep_submit_and_cancel`, `u3512c_picker_enter_does_not_submit_parent`, plus existing Capture/New Workstream submit and draft tests.
