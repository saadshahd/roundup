**U360 three Checks measure the rows' alignment, type and rhythm.** Found by #673: no Check read alignment. Narrows U137 and U136. Given `window.__checks()` (U137), then it returns three more entries, `D12`, `D13` and `D14`, each `{"status", "value", "selector"}` for what is on screen now, in the colour scheme the page is in, which `apps/desktop/src/testing/checks.ts` implements. A screen with no Shelf row passes all three.

- **D12 left edges.** In each of the `Todos` and `Pads` tabs, the Glyphs' left x are within 1 px of one another, the first words' left x are within 1 px of one another, and the left x of every line after a row's first is within 1 px of its row's title. The value is the largest difference measured, in px, with the selector of the row it was read on. Fails today: `tree-40` at 1280 by 820 has a Todo Glyph at 1140 and a Pad Glyph at 1117.
- **D13 font role.** In the Shelf, every text node's font family is `--font-ui` unless its element is an id (`#<id>`), a path (a Home line's path) or code; one that is `--font-mono` and none of those fails. The value is the count of offending nodes. Fails today: all of the Shelf's words are mono.
- **D14 row shape.** In the Shelf, no row has a non-transparent `background-color`, a border or a shadow at rest; the gap between neighbouring rows, and between the tablist and the list's first row is `0` or a space step and is equal across the two lists (U359's Rhythm; one list shows at a time, U373); no title or name shows more than two lines, and none overflows its row. The value is the first measure that fails. Fails today: a Todo row has `background-color: var(--ground)` and `border-radius: var(--radius-row)` (`rowButton.styles.css:63-64`).
