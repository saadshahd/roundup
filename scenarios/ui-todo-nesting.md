# UI: Todos nest by what they wait on (U3708 to U3712)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U3708 to U3712.

Webview only: no App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes; `todo.setBlockers` (T4), `todo.reorder` (T11) and `todo.complete` are called as `scenarios/todos.md` says. Issue #2897. The user said: "I want todos to be nested to reflect dependency - nested with indention and changing that order or nesting means changing dependency", and of the `Waits on` line: "This is ugly and misaligned". Settled with the user: nesting means sequence, so an indented Todo waits on the Todo above it. There is no parent field: a Todo's depth is read from its `blockers` (T3, T4) and from nothing else, so one fact never shows two ways.

```text
☐ Design the schema
   ☐ Write migrations   waits on parent
      ☐ Backfill data   waits on parent
```

Narrows U361 (its `Waits on` line is replaced by U3708 and U3712), U155 (a drag also sets depth) and U15 (the list is a tree). Amends nothing in `scenarios/todos.md`.

**U3708 the Todos list is a tree drawn from `blockers`.** _Amended only as listed by U3789e (`ui-shelf.md`); U3789a–d govern those replacement clauses._ Given the Shelf's open Todos (U15), when a test reads the rendered list on `tree-40`, `agents-10` and `first-run` (U26) and on U359's and U361's worst-case fixtures, light and dark for behavioral observers (captures and critic Check readings use dark only, U3725), then:

- **Nesting.** A Todo whose first open blocker is in the same list is drawn indented one level under that blocker, directly below it among that blocker's children, in the Todo order (T10); a Todo with no open blocker in the list is at level 1. A blocker that is done, or not in the list (another Home, U2744a's scope; an Agent's Todo, U60), does not nest: the Todo is drawn at level 1 and the list holds no parent field, no `parent` prop and no second source of depth. Test: `u3708_depth_is_read_from_the_first_blocker`.
- **Other blockers.** A Todo with several open blockers nests under its first, in `blockers` order (T4), and shows one quiet line `also waits on: <title>, <title>` naming the rest by title, in its own element `data-todo-also-waits`, one line with `…` and the whole line as the element's `title` (U359's Long text); each title is a click target opening that blocker's Drawer (U17) with the accessible name `open blocker <title>`, as U361 says. A Todo with one open blocker shows no such line. Test: `u3708_the_other_blockers_show_as_one_quiet_line`.
- **A tree for assistive tech.** The list is an ARIA tree built on `ui/TreeView` (Ark), never hand-written: the container has `role="tree"`, each row `role="treeitem"` with `aria-level` its depth and `aria-expanded` absent (rows are never folded); the tick box, the row button and U155's keys keep U361's paths, and Tab order is unchanged. Test: `u3708_the_list_is_a_tree_of_treeitems_with_levels`.
- **Ticking.** Checking a parent's tick box calls `todo.complete {id}` once (U361); when `todo.updated` and `todo.list` arrive, its children are no longer blocked by it (T3), draw at level 1 or under their next open blocker, and lose their `⏸`. Test: `u3708_ticking_a_parent_unnests_its_children`.

Observer: Vitest `u3708_` cases on the fake App seam; the design critic judges the indentation and the Checks (AGENTS.md rule 10). Moves: D2, D5, D6.

**U3709 Tab nests a row and ⇧Tab un-nests it.** Given the Shelf's list (U3708) with the row button of a Todo focused, when the user presses `Tab` (no modifier) and a Todo sits above it in the same list, then `todo.setBlockers {id, blockers}` is called once with the Todo above added as the first blocker and the existing blockers after it, and nothing else; when the user presses `⇧Tab` and the Todo has an open blocker in the list, then `todo.setBlockers` is called once with its first blocker removed; the row moves only when `todo.updated` arrives and `todo.list` is refetched (U15), and focus stays on the row's button (U138: never `body`). Both are plain keys scoped to the Shelf's list: they act only while focus is on a row button of the list, and elsewhere `Tab` moves focus as before, so U2241's allowlist is unchanged (it takes no modifier chord) and Tab never leaves a trap: on the first row, `Tab` calls nothing and moves focus on as the browser does; on a row at level 1, `⇧Tab` calls nothing and moves focus back. Tests: `u3709_tab_nests_under_the_row_above_and_calls_set_blockers_once`, `u3709_shift_tab_removes_the_first_blocker`, `u3709_tab_elsewhere_moves_focus_as_before`.

A refused call (U3711) leaves the row where it was and shows its cause. Observer: Vitest `u3709_` cases; the U26 harness presses the keys with `agent-browser` (headless, fresh profile) on `tree-40`'s three open Todos and reads the calls. Moves: D5.

**U3710 dragging sideways sets depth and dragging vertically sets order.** Narrows U155. Given the Shelf's list (U3708), when the user drags a row (U155's drag, started the same way, through dnd-kit's tree pattern, reusing `ui/sortable`'s sensors) and releases it, then the depth the pointer's sideways offset reaches, one level per indent step and bounded by the row above's level plus one and by level 1, sets its blockers, and the place its vertical position reaches sets its order:

- A drop at a new depth calls `todo.setBlockers {id, blockers}` once, the Todo above the drop as first blocker for a nest and the first blocker dropped for an un-nest; a drop at a new place calls `todo.reorder` once, as U155 says; a drop that changes both calls `todo.setBlockers` first and `todo.reorder` after it resolves; a drop that changes neither calls nothing. While dragging, the row's projected indent follows the pointer and no row but the dragged one changes place until the drop (U155's "place held").
- Dragging a Todo takes its descendants with it: they stay nested under it and are never given new blockers by the drop; `todo.reorder` is called for the dragged Todo alone and the Daemon's order (T10, T11) keeps its children below it as `todo.list` returns them.
- `Esc` and a release outside the list call nothing and settle the row back; U362's drop onto the Rail and U155's keyboard moves are unchanged. Reduced motion: U155's rule.

Tests: `u3710_a_sideways_drag_calls_set_blockers_and_a_vertical_one_calls_reorder`, `u3710_a_drag_that_changes_both_sets_blockers_then_reorders`, `u3710_depth_is_bounded_by_the_row_above`, `u3710_escape_and_a_release_outside_call_nothing`. Observer: the U26 harness drags `tree-40`'s rows with real pointer events in `agent-browser` (headless, fresh profile); captures at 1280 by 820 go to `artifacts/ux/U3710/`. Moves: D5, D6.

**U3711 a move that would make a cycle is refused and says why.** Given Todos 1 and 2 where 2 waits on 1, when a Tab, a ⇧Tab or a drop of U3709 or U3710 makes `todo.setBlockers` fail (T4: `CONFLICT` for a cycle or a self-block, `NOT_FOUND` for a Todo deleted meanwhile), then the list is unchanged as `todo.list` returns it, the row returns to its place (motion.md, "Drag to reorder"), and `✕ <message>` shows in place of the row's `also waits on` line (U35) with the Daemon's message, which names the Todos involved, until the next change to the list; focus stays on the row. The webview makes no cycle check of its own: the Daemon decides (T4). A `todo.reorder` that fails after a successful `todo.setBlockers` shows its message the same way and leaves the new depth. Tests: `u3711_a_refused_move_returns_the_row_and_shows_the_cause`, `u3711_the_webview_never_checks_for_a_cycle` (the fake App seam answers `CONFLICT`; the call is made, not suppressed).
