# UI: the visual system (U130 to U137, U161)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U130–U137, U161.

Webview only. No App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes. `docs/design-system.md` holds the Tokens, the six rules and the checks D1 to D10; each scenario below is the test that proves some of them, and its test name starts with its id. Every check runs on every visible element, in light and in dark, and fails when it cannot measure. `docs/motion.md` is unchanged.

These scenarios narrow U4 (ink): its Glyph tones for `working`, `idle` and `done`, its hex values and its `--light` and `--lightest` colours are replaced by `docs/design-system.md`. Its contrast ratios stay, as D5. Any `u4_`, `u5_` or `u20_` test that asserts a replaced colour is changed in the PR that replaces it, and the PR names each such test.

**U130 tokens.** Given every stylesheet under `apps/desktop/src` and every inline style a component sets, when a test reads them, then `tokens.css` is the only file with a colour, font-size, radius, box-shadow or duration literal; every other file reads `var(--…)` for those properties; and each Token in the table in `docs/design-system.md` is defined there, with a light and a dark value (a Token without both fails). xterm's options in `terminal/emulator.ts` are outside the literal scan; U131 moves its font size. Computed on a rendered Rail, Shelf, Drawer and pane, every colour, background, border-color, shadow, radius and duration is the value of a Token (D1). `loop/rules.sh tokens` (L41) repeats the literal scan in CI. Test: `u130_`. Built in slices, so no merged PR breaks the App in either scheme, and each says in its body which clauses of this scenario hold after it and `Moves: none` until D1 can pass: (1) `tokens.css` with each Token's light value, imported by `main.tsx` and the harness before `styles.css`; a Token that `styles.css` defines today moves out of it with the same value, so no name has two definitions (`u130_styles_css_does_not_shadow_a_shared_token`); the same PR adds `../tokens.css` to the stylesheet list in `u4_text_colour_rules_outside_glyph_tones_never_use_light_or_lightest`, makes `testing/contrast.ts` and `u4_the_word_hover_colour_is_text` resolve Tokens from `tokens.css`, and names both tests; the tests are one that each Token of the table is defined in `tokens.css` with a light value (`u130_every_token_has_the_tables_light_value_except_the_named_differences` asserts each Token's light value is the table's, except the differences it names in its `KNOWN_DIFFERENCES`; the table's values take effect where slice 2 moves a stylesheet, so `--text` becomes `#1d1d1f` in `styles.css`'s PR with the `u4_` tests that read it, and that PR deletes `--text` from `KNOWN_DIFFERENCES`; `testing/contrast.ts` reads `:root` from `tokens.css`) and a visible `it.todo` for the dark value; every stylesheet other than `styles.css` is untouched, and `u130_no_stylesheet_sets_a_dark_scheme_before_slice_3` reads each stylesheet's text and fails on `prefers-color-scheme` or `color-scheme`, which is what keeps the App light, and slice 3 deletes it. (2) One PR for each stylesheet that moves its literals to `var(--…)` and the inline styles of the components in its directory, and one more PR for the inline styles in directories with no stylesheet (`drawer/`, `pads/`, `todos/`, for example the Drawer's slide duration); the `PadDrawer` `color: var(--light)` is U50's, as U4 says. The Rail stylesheet's PR reads the selected-row band as `var(--selected)`, whose light value is `rgba(0,0,0,0.05)` in both the table and `tokens.css` (the table's earlier `.07` gave `--grey` text 4.34:1 on the band, below U4's 4.5:1), so no `KNOWN_DIFFERENCES` entry is needed for it, and `testing/contrast.ts` resolves the band through `var(--selected)`. **U131 type and space.** Given the same rendered screens, when a test reads computed style, then every font size is one of 11, 12, 13 or 15 px; every font family is `--font-ui` except the Terminal, a path, an id and code, which are `--font-mono`; and every margin, padding and gap is `0` or one of 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 or 32 px (D2). A Rail row is 28 px high at rest, hovered and selected (U46 still holds). So is the `✓ <n> done` fold line (U8): found at 15 px beside 28 px rows on `tree-40` at 252 px. Test: `u131_`.

**U132 colour and Kind.** Given a Rail with every Kind (seed `agents-10`) and a Rail with none of `error` and `needs-you`, when a test reads computed style, then each Glyph is the one `docs/design-system.md` assigns its Kind, in the tone it assigns; only `needs-you` and `error` rows are bold (Ink); every colour with saturation above 15% is `--red`, `--amber` or `--accent`; the second Rail draws no red and no amber (D3); and each row in the Rail, the Shelf and a Drawer shows one of the six Glyphs, so no Kind is drawn by colour alone (D4). Test: `u132_`.

**U133 click targets.** Given every element in the Rail, the Shelf, a Drawer, the header and the pane's pinned actions that is a click target (`docs/design-system.md`, "Click targets"), when a test drives it by `pointerenter`, `pointerdown` and `focus-visible`, then its hover style differs from its rest style in background or colour and its cursor is `pointer`; its pressed style differs from hover, takes effect at `pointerdown` and has `transition-duration: 0s`; its focus style is a 2 px `--accent` outline at 3:1 against its ground, drawn by one bare `:focus-visible` rule in `apps/desktop/src/styles.css` that every other stylesheet defers to (no stylesheet sets another outline colour, and none sets `outline: none`), so a Todo's title and body (U139), the Drawer's buttons and the Rail's rows and prompt field show the same ring; and its hit area is at least 24 px high (D6, D7). `stop`, `close`, `promote`, `collapse` and `export .md` are buttons by this rule. A disabled one has `--grey` text, cursor `default` and `aria-disabled` (U25). Test: `u133_`.

**U134 surfaces and depth.** Given the main workspace with a Drawer open, when a test reads computed style, then the Rail and the Shelf use `--sunken`, the pane and the Drawer use `--ground`, no border is wider than 1 px or any colour but `--hairline`, only the Drawer has a `box-shadow` (`--shadow-drawer`), no `backdrop-filter` element has another inside it, and the Terminal's size does not change when the Drawer opens (U5) (D8). Test: `u134_`.

**U135 schemes.** Given `prefers-color-scheme: dark`, `prefers-contrast: more`, `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce` and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, each alone, when a test renders the main workspace, then in dark every Token takes its dark value; under more contrast every `--grey` text computes as `--text` and every `--hairline` as 40% opaque; under reduced transparency no element has a `backdrop-filter`; under reduced motion every duration but the instant changes is `0s` (D9); and in every case text keeps 4.5:1 and every Glyph tone and focus ring 3:1 against its own ground (`done` exempt, as U4 says) (D5). D1 to D9 hold in each, and at 700 by 800 (D10). Test: `u135_`.

**U137 one module measures the checks.** Given the App in the harness (U26), when `window.__checks()` is evaluated, then it returns an object from each id `D1` to `D10` to `{"status": "pass" | "fail", "value": …, "selector": …}` for what is on screen now, in the colour scheme and media the page is in, computed by the same module the `u130_` to `u135_` tests call, so a check has one implementation. A check it cannot measure returns `fail` with the `value` `"not measurable"`, never `pass`. It reads the page and changes nothing the user sees: the state it drives to read a hover, press or focus style is restored. Test: `u137_`, which also asserts that every id is present, that a stylesheet with a literal colour makes D1 `fail`, and that the object parses under the `checks.json` shape `loop/rules.sh delta` reads (L42).

**U136 the existing screens pass.** Given the seeds `first-run`, `agents-10`, `tree-40`, `daemon-exits` and `conflict` (U26), when D1 to D10 run on each, then every check passes. This is the visual pass's finish line: it merges when `u130_` to `u135_` pass on those seeds, and QA's baseline (`docs/design-system.md`, "Baseline protocol") shows each check `fixed` and none `regressed`. Test: `u136_`.

**U161 the first window reads as designed.** Found by the user's report #355, a screenshot of the main window with one Workstream whose Door is stopped: the Rail's Workstream row, its `+ agent  + terminal  + workstream` row, the Shelf's `todos` and `pads` lists and the centre's `Door stopped` / `start Door` float apart with no rhythm, no line tells the Rail, the Shelf and the centre apart, and the marks look crude. Narrows U131, U134 and U143, and replaces two sentences in `docs/design-system.md` ("Surfaces and depth"): "Regions are told apart by that change of ground and by space, not by a drawn line" becomes "Regions are told apart by that change of ground and by space, and by one hairline on the Rail's trailing edge and one on the Shelf's top edge"; "The one border allowed is `1px solid var(--hairline)`, where a region meets one of the same ground" becomes "The only borders allowed are `1px solid var(--hairline)`, on the Rail's trailing edge and the Shelf's top edge, and the Drawer's leading edge (U134)". This is because `--sunken` against `--ground` alone did not separate them on screen. Changes no contract, App seam command or Token value; U86 and U130 to U137 keep holding. Given `?seed=door-stopped` (U143) at 1280 by 800 and at 700 by 800, in light and in dark, then:

- **Borders.** One 1 px `--hairline` line sits on the Rail's trailing edge (between the Rail and the centre) and one on the top edge of the Shelf where it meets the Rail's rows; no other border is drawn between regions, so D8 still passes. The Drawer keeps its own leading border and shadow (U134).
- **Rhythm.** Inside the Rail, the Workstream row, the add row and the Shelf's lists keep U131's 28 px rows and are separated only by `--space-2` or `--space-3` gaps; every region's inner padding is the same `--space-4` on its leading and trailing edges (the Rail's rows, the Shelf's `todos` and `pads` headings and the centre's content share one left edge per region); the add row's three words are one `--space-2` apart and their hit areas do not overlap (D6). The `pads` and `todos` headings and their first row are `--space-2` apart in both lists.
- **The centre's state.** The `Door stopped` line and `start Door` form one group centred in the pane both ways: the group's box centre is within 1 px of the pane's, the line is `--grey` at `--text-body`, the button is the primary action (`--accent`) with a 28 px hit height, and the line and the button are `--space-3` apart. The same holds for `no Workstream yet` and `start a Workstream` (U143) and for `starting Door…`, `Door did not start` and `Door not started`. Nothing in the centre resizes between those states (U143).
- **Marks.** Every mark in the Rail, the Shelf and the centre is an SVG from the library U86 names, drawn at one size (16 px) on one stroke width and one corner style, so the chevron, check, plus and Kind marks are one family; no two marks of the same meaning differ in size or weight between the Rail and the Shelf.
- **Checks.** `window.__checks()` (U137) passes D1 to D10 on this screen in both schemes and at both sizes, and no Check passing on `main` regresses. Asserted by `u161_checks`, which evaluates `window.__checks()` on the `door-stopped` seed in both schemes and at both sizes and compares it with `main`'s `checks.json` (L42).
