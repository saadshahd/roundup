# UI: buttons and the sidebar (U170 to U173)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U170–U173, U474–U475, U477, U483–U485, U498–U500, U535.

Webview only. No App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes. Found by the user's report #400: buttons are neither designed nor defined, and Solo's sidebar is the bar the user named, "elegant, well designed and tasteful". These scenarios narrow U133 (click-target states), U131 (type and space), U134 and U161 (surfaces and borders) and extend `docs/design-system.md` ("Click targets") with the button kinds below. A Workstream is drawn as a Room until #369's rename (U191, U163) merges; each clause holds under either name, and a test reads the name from the Rail. The reference image is not on file, so the observable bar is the list of what it shows, restated in U173.

**U170 five button kinds, each defined once.** Given `docs/design-system.md`, when a test renders the App, then there are exactly five button kinds: **primary** (`--accent` label, the one action of a state), **quiet** (`--grey` label at rest, `--text` on hover, no surface at rest), **add** (a `+` mark and a label, quiet, trailing a list or group), **row action** (a word or mark shown on a row's hover, focus or selection, and always reachable by keyboard), and **icon button** (a 16 px mark in a 24 px square, with an `aria-label`). Every `button`, `[role=button]` and click target in the Rail, Shelf, Drawer and centre carries one `data-button` attribute naming its kind, and a click target with none fails. A row is not a button: a `treeitem` row of the Rail, a Shelf row and a Todo's title (U139) are exempt, take no `data-button`, and are styled only by U172's Item, Selection and Workstream-row clauses; any button inside a row (its row action, its chevron) is still one of the five kinds. New Tokens (a pressed surface, a disabled label tone) are added to the table in `docs/design-system.md` with a light and a dark value, and U130 still holds. Test: `u170_`.

**U171 every kind shows every state.** Given the Tokens in both schemes for the disabled-behavior test and in dark only for critic Check readings (U3725), then the design critic judges that the disabled tone is 4.5:1 against the ground and the sunken surface and that the pressed surface differs from the hover surface (AGENTS.md rule 10). A disabled button stays focusable and keeps `aria-disabled`, never the `disabled` attribute, so its reason can be read as its `title`. Test: `u510_disabled_behavior_is_preserved`.

**U172 the sidebar's hierarchy.** Given the Rail and the Shelf on the seeds `agents-10` and `tree-40` (U26) in both schemes for non-capture observers (captures and critic Check readings use dark only, U3725), when a test reads the rendered tree and computed style, then:

> Amended by U403 and U404: the Workstream row has no badge, value or head and leads with its chevron, and the Item row's title is regular weight with no description line in the Rail.

- **Workstream row.** A Workstream row has, in order: a square badge of the Workstream's first two letters (uppercase, `--text-caption`, `--radius-row`, on `--selected`), its name in bold `--text-body` with the ellipsis of U29, a disclosure chevron, and a right-aligned value in `--grey` (the count of its Agents that are not `done`). The badge, name and value share one baseline of 28 px or taller; the row is `--space-3` from its neighbours.
- **Groups.** The Rail's Groups (U3) are collapsible; U373's tabs name the Shelf's lists and U2744a names their scope groups. Each group head has a 16 px mark, its label in uppercase `--text-caption` with letter-spacing, a `--hairline` rule filling the rest of the line, and a count `<shown>/<total>` in `--grey`. Collapsing a group hides its rows, keeps the head and its count, and survives a restart as U100 says. The head's label is a `GLOSSARY.md` term, never a synonym from the reference.
- **Item.** An item row has a Kind Glyph (U132, the status dot's role), a title, and below it a description. No key hint is drawn, since no chord addresses a single row; a hint is added only with such a chord. A row with no body draws no description. A `needs-you` or `error` row is told by colour and Glyph, never by weight (amended: no row is bold in Ink; U132): the title weight above is the item default and Ink is told by tone and Glyph, so from the implementing PR on, U172's title clause replaces U132's "only `needs-you` and `error` rows are bold" clause; that PR rewrites U132 and changes `u132_` in the same change, and names both in its body.
