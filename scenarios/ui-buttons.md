# UI: buttons and the sidebar (U170 to U173)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U170–U173.

Webview only. No App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes. Found by the user's report #400: buttons are neither designed nor defined, and Solo's sidebar is the bar the user named, "elegant, well designed and tasteful". These scenarios narrow U133 (click-target states), U131 (type and space), U134 and U161 (surfaces and borders) and extend `docs/design-system.md` ("Click targets") with the button kinds below. A Workstream is drawn as a Room until #369's rename (U162, U163) merges; each clause holds under either name, and a test reads the name from the Rail. The reference image is not on file, so the observable bar is the list of what it shows, restated in U173.

**U170 five button kinds, each defined once.** Given `docs/design-system.md`, when a test reads its "Click targets" section and every stylesheet under `apps/desktop/src`, then the section defines exactly five kinds: **primary** (`--accent` label, the one action of a state), **quiet** (`--grey` label at rest, `--text` on hover, no surface at rest), **add** (a `+` mark and a label, quiet, trailing a list or group), **row action** (a word or mark shown on a row's hover, focus or selection, and always reachable by keyboard), and **icon button** (a 16 px mark in a 24 px square, with an `aria-label`). Each kind's rest, hover, pressed, focus and disabled style is a pure function of Tokens: one rule per kind in one stylesheet, `buttons.css`, and no other stylesheet sets a `background`, `color`, `border-radius` or `padding` on a `button` element. Every `button`, `[role=button]` and click target in the Rail, Shelf, Drawer, Thread and centre carries one `data-button` attribute naming its kind, and a click target with none fails. A row is not a button: a `treeitem` row of the Rail, a Shelf row and a Todo's title (U139) are exempt, take no `data-button`, and are styled only by U172's Item, Selection and Workstream-row clauses; any button inside a row (its row action, its chevron) is still one of the five kinds. New Tokens (a pressed surface, a disabled label tone) are added to the table in `docs/design-system.md` with a light and a dark value, and U130 still holds. Test: `u170_`.

**U171 every kind shows every state.** Given the five kinds rendered together on a fixture page in light and in dark, when a test drives each by `pointerenter`, `pointerdown`, `focus-visible` and `aria-disabled`, then for each kind: hover differs from rest in background or colour; pressed differs from hover, takes effect at `pointerdown` and has `transition-duration: 0s`; focus is the one `:focus-visible` ring of U133, never clipped by its parent (the ring's box lies inside the nearest clipping ancestor, or that ancestor does not clip it); disabled has the disabled tone at 4.5:1 against its ground, cursor `default` and no hover or pressed change; the hit area is at least 24 px high and no two hit areas in one row overlap (D6, D7). A disabled button stays focusable and keeps `aria-disabled`, never the `disabled` attribute, so its reason can be read as its `title`. The kinds differ from one another: no two kinds have the same rest style. Test: `u171_`.

**U172 the sidebar's hierarchy.** Given the Rail and the Shelf on the seeds `agents-10` and `tree-40` (U26) in both schemes, when a test reads the rendered tree and computed style, then:

- **Workstream row.** A Workstream row has, in order: a square badge of the Workstream's first two letters (uppercase, `--text-caption`, `--radius-row`, on `--selected`), its name in bold `--text-body` with the ellipsis of U29, a disclosure chevron, and a right-aligned value in `--grey` (the count of its Agents that are not `done`). The badge, name and value share one baseline of 28 px or taller; the row is `--space-3` from its neighbours.
- **Groups.** The Rail's Groups (U3) and the Shelf's `todos` and `pads` lists are collapsible, and share one head. Each group head has a 16 px mark, its label in uppercase `--text-caption` with letter-spacing, a `--hairline` rule filling the rest of the line, and a count `<shown>/<total>` in `--grey`. Collapsing a group hides its rows, keeps the head and its count, and survives a restart as U100 says; from the implementing PR on, U100's persisted collapse covers the Shelf's two lists too, and that PR rewrites U100 and its test to say so. The head's label is a `GLOSSARY.md` term, never a synonym from the reference.
- **Item.** An item row has a Kind Glyph (U132, the status dot's role), a title in bold `--text-body`, and below it a description in `--grey` `--text-caption` clamped to two lines with `…`. No key hint is drawn, since no chord addresses a single row; a hint is added only with such a chord. A row with no description is 28 px as U131 says; a row with one grows to hold it and never overlaps the next. A `needs-you` or `error` row is still the only bold-in-Ink row (U132): the title weight above is the item default and Ink is told by tone and Glyph, so from the implementing PR on, U172's title clause replaces U132's "only `needs-you` and `error` rows are bold" clause; that PR rewrites U132 and changes `u132_` in the same change, and names both in its body.
- **Selection.** The selected row has one mark: a `--selected` band with a 2 px `--accent` bar on its leading edge, drawn as a background (a pseudo-element or gradient), never a border (D8). No other row has a band or bar. Hover is `--hover` and never reads as selected.
- **Blocks.** One `--hairline` line sits between each block of the Rail (the Workstream rows, each group); U161's two lines remain, and the implementing PR widens U134's border rule in `docs/design-system.md` and in `scenarios/ui-visual.md` to name them. No border is wider than 1 px or any colour but `--hairline` (D8).
- **Density.** Rows have at least `--space-2` of vertical padding and `--space-4` of horizontal padding, the Rail stays 252 px wide, and `tree-40` at 252 px shows no horizontal scroll, no overlap and no clipped label (D10, U85).

Test: `u172_` against the fake App seam: structure, order and text by role and `data-` attribute, style by computed value, the persisted collapse by remounting. Jsdom computes no layout, so the sizes, overlap and clipping are read on `just harness` in `agent-browser` at 1280 by 800 and 700 by 800, with each box reported.

**U173 the sidebar reads as elegant, well designed and tasteful.** Given the sidebar after U170 to U172 on `agents-10` and `tree-40`, in both schemes, when the Reviewer (a fresh run holding the issue's reference list below, never the author's rationale) opens `just harness` in `agent-browser` and captures the Rail and the Shelf, then it answers each line with a measurement or capture, and the clause fails when any line is "no" or missing:

1. Surfaces are dark in dark scheme, with a thin border between blocks.
2. A Workstream row reads badge, bold name, chevron, quiet value.
3. A group head reads mark, spaced uppercase label, hairline rule, count.
4. An item reads dot, bold title, two-line grey description. It draws no key hint, an intended omission (U172), not a "no".
5. Row height and padding are generous, and the spacing is even.
6. One selected-row mark is clear at a glance.
7. Every button of the sidebar is one of the five kinds (U170) and shows its states (U171).
8. Beside the reference's attributes, the Reviewer states in one line whether the whole reads elegant, well designed and tasteful, and names the one thing that most spoils it, if any. That line is a `NOTE:` that a fix run reads; lines 1 to 7 decide.
