# UI: the Shelf's Todos group by owner (U3717 to U3720)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Acceptance for #2896. Needs T16 and T17 (#2894: `owner` on a Todo, `todo.assign`, `todo.create {owner}`). Replaces the Todos tab's grouping by creator in U2744a (Overview placement, Order and Folding clauses, for Todos only; Pads keep U2744a's Home and maker groups, and the Scope clause is unchanged). No RPC, App seam command or Token changes; `todo.assign` and `todo.create`'s `owner` are T17's and T16's.

**U3717 the Todos tab groups by owner.** _Amended only as listed by U3789e (`ui-shelf.md`); U3789a–d govern those replacement clauses._ Given the Shelf's Todos tab (U373) in any scope of U2744a, then the open Todos in scope are grouped by `owner` (T16), once each: Me (`Actor::user()`), then one group per Agent on the Rail that owns one, in Rail order, then Unassigned (`owner: null`, or an Agent no longer on the Rail). Within a group Todos keep T10 order. A group holding no Todo shows no header, except that dragging (U3718) or adding (U3719) names every owner: see there. Folding follows U2744a's Folding clause (shared TreeView, Unassigned starts folded, counts unchanged by folding). Done Todos stay in the `✓ n done` fold of U155, in the same scope. Scope still follows U2744a (Home and maker select which Todos are in scope; owner only groups them). The group label reads Me, the Agent's current Rail name, or Unassigned (user labels). An owner Agent that stops keeps its group (T17). Tests: `u3717_todos_group_by_owner_once_each`, `u3717_group_order_is_me_agents_unassigned`, `u3717_unassigned_starts_folded`, `u3717_owner_event_regroups_the_row` (a `todo.updated` and refetch moves the row), `u3717_scope_still_selects_by_home_and_maker`.

**U3718 drag a Todo to another owner's group.** _Amended only as listed by U3789e (`ui-shelf.md`); U3789a–d govern those replacement clauses._ Narrows U152, U155 and U362. Given the Todos tab with at least two groups, then pressing a row button and moving 4 px or more starts a drag through `ui/sortable.tsx` (the dnd-kit Sortable of U155; no new drag library or state machine, policy) and releasing over another group calls `todo.assign {id, owner}` once, with that group's owner (`Actor::user()`, the Agent's Actor, or `null` for Unassigned) and nothing else. While a drag is live every owner of the scope is a drop target (Me, each Agent on the Rail in scope, Unassigned), including a folded group, an empty one and one with no Todo yet; the group under the pointer shows it will take the Todo (`data-drop-target`, a Token-only look judged by the design critic). A drop on the Todo's own group reorders as U155 says and calls `todo.reorder`, never `todo.assign`; a drop on another group's row places by owner only (T10 order is not changed by it). The row stays where it was until `todo.updated` arrives and `todo.list` is refetched (U15), then sits in its new group. Esc or a drop outside every group cancels with no call. A refused reassign (`NOT_FOUND`, `CONFLICT`, any error) keeps the row in its group and shows `✕ <message>` in place of the row's `Waits on` line (U35), as U155's failed reorder does. Blocked rows and a dragged row's U362 Rail drop (Move to a Workstream) keep working; a drop on the Rail never changes the owner. The `✓ n done` fold takes no drag. Tests (`u3718_`, fake App seam answering `todo.assign` as T17 does): `u3718_a_drop_on_another_group_assigns_once`, `u3718_every_owner_is_a_drop_target_while_dragging` (folded, empty, Me, Unassigned), `u3718_a_drop_on_the_own_group_reorders_and_never_assigns`, `u3718_esc_and_an_outside_drop_call_nothing`, `u3718_a_refused_assign_keeps_the_row_and_shows_its_cause`, `u3718_a_rail_drop_moves_home_and_keeps_the_owner`, `u3718_the_row_moves_only_after_the_refetch`.

**U3719 each group header has a plus that adds a Todo to that group.** _Amended only as listed by U3789e (`ui-shelf.md`); U3789a–d govern those replacement clauses._ Narrows U2744a's Add clause. Given a group header of the Todos tab (Me, an Agent, Unassigned), then it holds, on its right, a plus button with accessible name `add todo to <group label>`, a 24 px hit area (D6), reachable by Tab and pressed by Enter or Space, and not part of the disclosure's own control (folding stays U2744a's). Pressing it opens the new-Todo field (the Shelf's existing add field, U15, U35) inside that group, opening the group if it is folded, with focus in the field; Esc or leaving it empty closes it with no call and returns focus to the plus. Committing a title calls `todo.create {title, owner}` once with that group's owner (`Actor::user()`, the Agent's Actor, or `null`), then follows U2744a's Add clause for the Home (`todo.move` with the selected Workstream or Agent's Workstream; Overview adds at Project root); the new Todo shows in that group after the refetch. Only one new-Todo field is open at a time; opening another closes the first, discarding its text. A failed create keeps the field open with the typed text and shows `✕ <message>` inside the group (U152's failed-commit rule). An Agent group whose Agent has left the Rail since the field opened fails as `NOT_FOUND` (T16) and shows that. An empty Todos tab keeps its existing add path, which creates an Unassigned Todo. Tests (`u3719_`): `u3719_the_plus_creates_in_that_groups_owner` (Me, an Agent, Unassigned: exact `todo.create` params), `u3719_the_plus_opens_a_folded_group_and_focuses_the_field`, `u3719_esc_closes_and_returns_focus_to_the_plus`, `u3719_a_failed_create_keeps_the_text_and_shows_its_cause`, `u3719_the_plus_is_keyboard_reachable_with_its_name`, `u3719_the_plus_never_toggles_the_fold`.

**U3720 a plain key opens the owner picker for the focused row.** Narrows U152 (every Todo action has a keyboard path, here `Assign`) and obeys U2241. Given the Todos tab with a Todo row button focused, then the plain key `o` (no `⌘`, `⌃`, `⌥`, no `⇧`; registered once in the registry's Shelf scope and listed in Help and the key hints of U2541b for the Shelf place, never a local `keydown` listener, policy) opens one list of owners as a popover over the Shelf below the row (it never resizes the Shelf or moves a row): `Me`, each Agent on the Rail in Rail order, `Unassigned`, the Todo's current owner left out. Each offer is reachable by Tab or the arrow keys and chosen by Enter or Space; choosing calls `todo.assign {id, owner}` once and nothing else. Esc, a click outside or choosing an offer closes it and focus returns to the row button (found by `#<id>` after the refetch), never `body` (U138). The key does nothing while focus is in a text field, the Drawer's editor or the new-Todo field, on a done Todo's row, outside the Todos tab, or with a modifier held (`⌥O`, `⌃O`, `⌘O` reach their existing owners unprevented). A row also keeps a pointer path: the row's light `Assign` word beside `Move` (U157), shown on hover or focus, out of the flow, 24 px hit area (D6), accessible name `assign #<id>`, opening the same list, so U112 holds (no row changes its top edge, height or wrapping). A failure shows `✕ <message>` in place of the row's `Waits on` line and the Todo stays in its group. Tests (`u3720_`): `u3720_o_opens_the_owner_list_without_the_current_owner`, `u3720_choosing_assigns_once_and_returns_focus_to_the_row`, `u3720_esc_and_click_outside_close_and_restore_focus`, `u3720_o_is_inert_in_text_fields_with_modifiers_and_on_done_rows`, `u3720_a_failed_assign_shows_its_cause_and_keeps_the_group`, `u3720_the_assign_word_opens_the_same_list`, `u3720_the_key_is_a_registered_shelf_binding_listed_in_help` (the U2241 sweep and Help tests keep passing, with the new row).

Policy — implementation boundary: `apps/desktop/src/shelf/`, `todos/` and `copy.ts`, `keys/` (one registry row and its Help and hint entries), `ui/sortable.tsx` only if it lacks a cross-list drop target (extend, never replace), and the explicitly named regression callers. Todo `creator` no longer groups; U2744a's Todo placement tests (`u2744a_shelf_scope_overview_groups_every_item_once`, `u2744a_shelf_scope_empty_groups_are_omitted`, `u2744a_shelf_scope_unassigned_starts_folded` and the Todo cases of `u2744a_shelf_scope_counts_follow_the_scope`) change in the implementation PR to assert U3717's groups, each cut naming the U3717 test that proves its then-clause (AGENTS.md rule 10); Pad grouping tests stay. New user labels (Me, Unassigned, `add todo to <group>`, `Assign`) go in `copy.ts`.

It drags a Todo from Unassigned to an Agent's group and back to Me by the pointer, assigns another by `o` and the keyboard alone, and adds one through each group's plus; it passes when `todo.list`'s `owner` equals the group the row reads in after each step and after reload, `document.activeElement` is never `body`, and a refused assign (the Agent removed mid-drag) keeps the row and shows its cause.
