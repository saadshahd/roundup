**U59 the terminal blends with the app.** (amended clause) In each colour scheme the emulator's `background` and `cursorAccent` are that scheme's `--ground`, its `foreground` and `cursor` that scheme's `--text`, and its `selectionBackground` that scheme's `--selected`; when the scheme changes while a Terminal is open, the emulator repaints with the new values without a reload and keeps its scrollback.
