# UI: Todos track progress, plan steps and dependency groups

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U3768 to U3773, U3770f, U3808 to U3811.

U3768–U3773 and U3770f are webview only: no App command, no Daemon method and nothing under `contracts/` changes; `todo.complete` and `todo.reopen` are called as U361 and U461a say. U3808's missing read seam is identified below; this specification changes no contract. Requested by #3300: the Shelf's Todos look and move like AffectUI's Progress Tracker (https://www.affectui.com/components/progress-tracker, free, MIT; its source, `https://www.affectui.com/r/progress-tracker.json`, lists `"dependencies": []`). Only its CSS ideas are used; the item is React-only, so no code is copied and the PR credits it. Narrows U361 (the tick box's look), U3010c (Todo motion) and `docs/design-system.md`'s direction sentence. U3010c's 150 ms fold and row slide, U53 and U156 keep their behaviour.

Decided here: the list is the Todos tab's list in the Shelf's current scope (U2744a), once; owner groups (U3717) get no bar of their own. The total is every Todo in that scope, open and done; the done count is those with `done`. A scope with no Todo shows no bar.

**U3768 the Todos list shows a progress bar with a done/total count above it.** Given the Shelf's Todos tab on `tree-40`, `agents-10` and `first-run` (U26), light and dark for non-capture observers (captures and critic Check readings use dark only, U3725), at 1280 by 820 and 1280 by 800, when a test reads the rendered tree, then:

- **Bar.** Above the first group, one Ark UI `Progress` (linear; wrapped in `apps/desktop/src/ui/`, not hand-written) with role `progressbar`, `aria-valuemin` 0, `aria-valuemax` the total, `aria-valuenow` the done count, and the accessible name `Todos done`. Its fill is drawn with `transform: scaleX(done/total)`, never an animated width. Test: `u3768_the_bar_reads_done_over_total`.
- **Count.** Beside the bar the text `<done>/<total>` in the quiet secondary type. It changes only when `todo.updated` arrives and `todo.list` is refetched (U15); a tick that has not been refetched or has failed (U35) leaves the bar and count as they were. Test: `u3768_the_count_follows_the_refetched_list`.
- **Empty.** A scope with no Todo shows neither bar nor count and reserves no space for them; one with Todos and none done shows `0/<total>` and an empty fill. Test: `u3768_an_empty_scope_shows_no_bar`.
- **Kept.** The bar and count take no focus, hold no button and call nothing; `todo.*` calls of every row action are unchanged and U112 holds (no row's top edge moves because a pointer hovers). Test: `u3768_the_bar_calls_nothing_and_takes_no_focus`.

Observer: Vitest `u3768_` cases on the fake App seam; captures at 1280 by 820 go to `artifacts/ux/U3768/`. Moves: D1, D2, D5, D6.

**U3769 the tick box draws its tick; it is still the one native control.** Narrows U361's Tick box. Given the open and done Todos of U3768's seeds, when a test reads the rendered tree and computed style, then the tick box (`todos/TickBox.tsx`, `rowButton.styles.css`) is still the native `input type="checkbox"` of U361 with its accessible name, 24 px hit area, visible focus, Space to toggle and tab order; no second checkbox exists. Its tick is drawn by an SVG path or a CSS mask on that input's box, as a stroke whose dash offset runs from full (hidden) to zero (drawn), and its ring is the same box's outline or border; a native checkbox glyph cannot draw a stroke, which is why the box is restyled. A done Todo mounted done shows the drawn tick and the settled ring with no motion; an open one shows neither tick nor offset stroke. The ticks keep D3's contrast on both states and the box stays 16 px. Tests: `u3769_the_tick_is_the_one_native_checkbox`, `u3769_a_done_row_mounts_with_the_tick_drawn`. Observer: Vitest `u3769_` cases; the rendered stroke is the design critic's (rule 10). Moves: D1, D2, D3, D5.

**U3770 a finishing Todo plays one motion; nothing else on the Shelf moves.** Given the Shelf showing the Todos tab (U3768), when:

- **User ticks or unticks.** The user ticks an open Todo (click or Space): once `todo.updated` arrives and `todo.list` is refetched (U15), that row draws its tick, pops its ring, grows a strike across its label, the bar fill grows to the new `done/total` and the count rolls in. Unticking (U461a) plays the same in reverse. The ordering and delays within it are the Tokens' of U3771. Test: `u3770_ticking_plays_the_tick_ring_strike_bar_and_count`, `u3770_unticking_plays_the_reverse`.
- **An Agent finishes a visible Todo.** `todo.updated` arrives for a Todo in the shown scope that an Agent completed (not the user's own tick): its row and the bar play the same motion. A Todo outside the scope or inside a folded group plays none and the bar and count still settle to the new values with no motion. Test: `u3770_an_agent_finishing_a_shown_todo_plays_the_motion`, `u3770_a_finish_out_of_view_moves_nothing`.
- **No motion at rest.** Opening the Shelf, a Rail switch (U2744a's scope change), first mount and a refetch that changes no `done` state show the end state at once: every done row with its tick, strike and ring settled, the bar at its final fill, the count at its final text; no row stagger, no card rise, no auto-play and no shine sweep. The marker the observer reads is `data-motion="play"` on a row or bar, present only while a motion plays and never on mount. Tests: `u3770_opening_and_switching_show_the_end_state_without_motion`, `u3770_a_refetch_with_no_change_plays_nothing`.
- **A burst retargets.** Several Todos finish in one burst, or a second finish arrives mid-motion: no motion queues, none restarts from the start, and each running one retargets from its current state to the new one, so the bar fill and count reach the last `done/total` without passing back through a stale one, and every row ends settled. Test: `u3770_a_burst_retargets_and_ends_on_the_last_state`.
- **Reduced motion.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the end state appears at once for each of the above: no `data-motion="play"` that moves anything, durations 0 (D9, `docs/design-system.md` heading **The checks**, `apps/desktop/src/testing/checks.ts` function `runChecks`), and the preference changing mid-motion settles it at the next frame as `u5_a_change_of_the_preference_is_followed` in `apps/desktop/src/app/reducedMotion.test.tsx` holds for other motion. Test: `u3770_reduced_motion_shows_the_end_state`.
- **Kept.** Ticking is one key or click with the `todo.*` calls of U361 and U461a unchanged; focus follows U138; the done fold and the row slide of U3010c keep their 150 ms behaviour and tests (`u138_*`, `u155_*`, `u3010c_*` run unchanged). Test: `u3770_the_calls_and_focus_are_unchanged` (the cited cases, run unchanged).

**U3773 ticking a scope's only open Todo keeps its row on screen while it finishes.** Narrows U3770's user tick. Given the Shelf's Todos tab (U3768) in a scope with exactly one open Todo and no row being added, when the user ticks it (click or Space) and `todo.updated` arrives and `todo.list` is refetched (U15), then the row stays rendered in its group through the whole motion of U3770, drawing its tick, ring and strike, and only after the bar settles does it leave for the done fold; at no frame between the tick and the fold does the scope show no row for that Todo. **U3770f a finish inside a folded group plays no motion.** Narrows U3770's agent finish and its user tick. A Todo in an unfolded group of the same scope still plays U3770's motion. Tests: `u3770f_a_finish_inside_a_folded_group_plays_no_motion`, `u3770f_a_finish_in_an_unfolded_group_still_plays`. **U3771 the motion reads its timings from Tokens.** Narrows U3010a's one-easing-source rule. `apps/desktop/src/tokens.css` gains, with no literal elsewhere (D1, D2): `--ease-todo` (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`), `--ease-pop` (an overshooting curve for the ring pop), and one duration for each effect: tick draw, ring pop, strike grow, bar fill, count roll, plus the delays of the tick and the strike. `--duration-todo` (150 ms) stays and keeps the fold and the row slide. Starting values are AffectUI's, and the design critic accepts or shortens them in the implementing PR; the PR adds a row for each to `docs/motion.md` and the Tokens to `docs/design-system.md`'s Tokens table, so D9 (each duration within 25% of `docs/motion.md`) holds.

| Effect | Starting value |
|---|---|
| tick draws, stroke dash offset 1 to 0 | 420 ms, 160 ms delay |
| ring pops, scale 0.9, 1.06, 1 | 560 ms |
| strike grows, background size 0 to 100% | 620 ms, 220 ms delay |
| bar fills, `scaleX` | 1100 ms |
| count rolls in | 560 ms |

Plain CSS only: no motion library and no JavaScript timer drives any of it; a transition or CSS animation on `transform`, `opacity`, `stroke-dashoffset` and `background-size` only, never `transition: all` (U3010a). Tests: `u3771_no_literal_duration_or_ease_outside_tokens` (D1 on the rendered Todos), `u3771_the_effects_resolve_the_token_durations`. Policy: the final values are the design critic's. Moves: D1, D9.

**U3772 the direction sentence and the worst case.** `docs/design-system.md`'s direction changes "nothing that moves unless the user did something" to "nothing moves unless the user acted or an Agent finished a Todo the user can see" (policy; the design critic and review read the document). Given U3768's seeds and U359's and U361's worst-case fixtures (300-character title, a one-letter, emoji and non-Latin title, 0 and 200 Todos, all done, none done), at a Shelf width of 163 px and of 480 px, then the bar and count never wrap or clip, the count shows `200/200` and (and no bar for an empty scope, U3768) without moving a row, and the design critic judges that D2, D5, D6, D9, D10 and D12 pass with no Check passing on `main` regressing (AGENTS.md rules 8 and 10). Cold start and keystroke budgets hold (rule 7, `docs/perf.md`). Tests: `u3768_the_bar_reads_done_over_total` runs on the harness's `Demo data / Worst case` toggle (`break-ui`); captures in `artifacts/ux/U3772/`. Moves: D2, D5, D6, D9, D10, D12.

**U3808 an Agent's Todos show the handed step as now.** _Amended only as listed by U3789e (`ui-shelf.md`); U3789a–d govern those replacement clauses._ Narrows T10, T25 and T26 (`scenarios/todos.md`), U3717's owner grouping (`scenarios/ui-shelf-owner.md`) and U3769's tick. Given one Agent's Todos in the Shelf's current scope, including done, open unblocked, open blocked and unhanded Todos, when the initial read or U15's event/refetch completes, then:

- **Facts.** Done means only `Todo.done`; done rows retain U3769's checked tick. For each open, unblocked Todo owned by this Agent, a qualifying Hand-off is a `Change.handed` naming its current owner after its last owner-changing Change (T26). Of qualifying Todos choose the one whose qualifying Hand-off has the greatest `Change.seq`; it alone shows the word `now` and `aria-current="step"` on its treeitem. If several Todos were handed and left open, the latest qualifying Hand-off wins; neither Todo order nor an Agent's Kind alone proves a current step. Other open steps stay unchecked with no `aria-current`; Me and Unassigned have no `now`. With no qualifying fact, including legacy history without `handed`, show no `now`. This denotes the latest handed work, not proof of what the vendor program is executing. Test: `u3808_now_uses_the_latest_eligible_handoff` (fixtures include two handed open Todos, blocked/done exclusions and legacy history).
- **Refresh and order.** Completion removes `now` from that Todo on accepted refetch; reopening alone creates no new Hand-off. Reassignment invalidates the previous owner's Hand-off, including an away-and-back assignment; a subsequent valid Hand-off may mark it again. Never change `done`, blockers, ownership, the T10 order or any write call to choose a marker. Preserve relative order within the existing owner/tree/done partitions; do not sort by marker, Kind or completion time. Test: `u3808_refetch_updates_now_without_reordering_or_writing` (non-id Todo order, completion, reopen, reassignment away/back and a later Hand-off).

Data seam on main: `crates/contracts/src/todo.rs` types `Todo`, `Change`, `History`; `crates/todos/src/store.rs` functions `hand` and `handed_to`; `crates/todos/src/handoff.rs` function `hand` emits `todo.updated` even though the Todo's values are unchanged. `Change.seq` orders the Project's Changes; compare it, not timestamps or terminal text. `apps/desktop/src/todos/state.ts` function `createTodosState` reads `todo.list` only; that reply has no Hand-off fact. History is fetched by the open Drawer under U462, not by each list row. Policy: the implementation prerequisite, tracked in #3454, is a bounded authoritative read of the existing Hand-off/owner-change facts for the current Project's list, refreshed on those same Events, without per-row History reads, Touches, polling, a new stored completion state or a new Message. That read must distinguish unknown from no qualifying Hand-off; unknown shows no `now`. Its Contract PR changes all callers/fakes together and probes initial read, unchanged-value Hand-off Event, reassignment and restart through the real Daemon; named test `u3808_the_list_reads_recorded_handoffs_without_history_or_touches`. T25's handing/delivery authority and laws M1–M10 remain unchanged. Existing proof narrowed: `crates/todos/tests/t26_handoff.rs` tests `t26_a_handoff_adds_one_change_with_equal_values`, `t26_old_changes_read_no_handed_actor`; `crates/todos/src/lib.rs` test `t10_completing_keeps_the_place_and_deleting_leaves_the_rest`.

**U3809 a nested dependency group keeps its members and progress when they finish.** _Amended only as listed by U3789e (`ui-shelf.md`); U3789a–d govern those replacement clauses._ Amends U3708's open-only parent choice, never-folded branches and parent-completion unnesting (`scenarios/ui-todo-nesting.md`), and U15/U3717's global done-fold placement only for members retained inside a dependency group (`scenarios/ui.md`, `scenarios/ui-shelf-owner.md`); narrows U3768 without giving owner groups bars. Given one owner group in the Shelf scope with Todos P, A, B, C in the user's order, A and B naming P as their first blocker in this group and C naming A, when the accepted list is rendered, then:

- **Membership.** Compute the parent from the first blocker in this same owner group among all its Todos, open and done. No separate parent field or persisted membership exists. P's group contains A, B and C once each, excludes P itself and excludes Todos in other owner groups/scopes and secondary-blocker links; A's subgroup contains only C. Done blockers and done children remain members, so finishing P does not discard its group, change membership or promote its children. Siblings retain T10 order and the tree retains its depth-first traversal. Deletion, scope/owner changes and accepted blocker edits recompute this membership from facts, never from a cached list of previously visible rows. Test: `u3809_membership_counts_each_descendant_once_across_completion` (also secondary and out-of-group blockers, move, reassignment and deletion).
- **Bar.** Each parent with at least one descendant has one additional Ark UI linear Progress, named `Todos done in <parent title>`, with min 0, max its descendant count and now its done descendant count, plus `<done>/<total>`. In the fixture, A done and B/C open gives P `1/3` and A `0/1`; all three done gives P `3/3`. It counts closed descendants too. No descendants means no disclosure or group bar. Owner headers still have no bar; U3768's one scope bar still counts each Todo once, including P, irrespective of folding. Test: `u3809_group_and_scope_progress_have_distinct_membership`. Policy: fills use U3768's `scaleX`; Tokens, contrast and text fit are the critic's observations.
- **Disclosure.** The parent has a separate named button `Toggle <parent title> group`, `aria-controls` its content and `aria-expanded` matching the treeitem's expanded state. Pointer click and Enter/Space toggle only that group, call no RPC and never tick P or open its Drawer. Reuse `ui/TreeView` (Ark) for branches; keep U361's one native tick and row action paths. Closed descendants are inert, absent from keyboard navigation and the accessibility tree, and cannot open Drawers or invoke tick/drag actions. The disclosure remains reachable. Test: `u3809_disclosure_toggles_only_its_inert_contents`.
- **Done rows and focus.** A member stays once in its group, including when done; it is not duplicated in U15's global done fold. This also amends U3770/U3773's destination for a finishing member to its retained group contents, without shortening its visible finish or bar settlement. A standalone done Todo keeps that fold. A completed group's header remains discoverable and can be manually reopened to inspect or untick a child. For a manual close, if the group would hide focus, move it to that group's disclosure before making descendants inert (U3811 governs completion focus); focus elsewhere is never stolen. Test: `u3809_closed_groups_preserve_focus_and_done_rows_are_not_duplicated`.

**U3811 completing the last child closes its group and reveals the next newly unblocked group.** Narrows U3770's visible completion, burst and rest rules, U3773's finishing-row retention and U3010c's final-identity structural motion (`scenarios/ui-motion.md`).
