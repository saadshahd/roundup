# UI: the keyboard reaches everything (U138 to U141)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U138 to U141. Found by ux-auditor-1's keyboard sweep of main (harness `tree-40`).

**U138 completing a Todo by keyboard keeps focus.** Given an open Todo row whose `complete` has focus (U35, reached by Tab from the row), when the user presses Enter or Space on it and the Todo leaves the open list, then focus moves to the row that took its place: the next open Todo's row, else the previous one, else the `+` button of the `todos` list. Focus is never left on the page body. A failed `todo.complete` keeps focus on `complete`. Tests (`u138_`): the middle, the last and the only open Todo each, and the failure case.

**U139 a Todo's title and body edit by keyboard.** Given a Todo's Drawer (U17), then its title and its body are each reachable by Tab, and Enter on the focused text starts editing it exactly as a double-click does (U17: the field takes focus, leaving it commits once). The title's own Enter still commits (U17); in the body field Enter adds a line and leaving commits, as with the pointer. The only look change is the focus ring of the new tab stop, which is the browser's own (one pixel outside the text); U133's one focus ring (2 px `--accent`, offset 1 px) replaces it when U133 is built. Tests (`u139_`): the title and the body are tab stops, and Enter on each opens its field with focus in it.

**U140 F2 renames the focused row.** Narrows U41 ("`F2` edits the selected name"). Found: after `↓` moves focus from the selected row to another, F2 renamed the selected row, which the user was no longer looking at. Given focus on a Rail row, then `F2` edits the name of the focused row in place, as double-click does (U9), whether or not that row is selected; with a click, focus and selection are the same row, so nothing changes there. With focus on a row's `collapse` or `promote` button, or in the pane, `F2` does nothing to the Rail, as U41 says. `u41_f2_edits_the_selected_rows_name_even_when_a_different_row_has_focus` is replaced by `u140_f2_edits_the_focused_rows_name_even_when_another_row_is_selected`, and `u41_f2_does_nothing_with_no_selection` by `u140_f2_edits_the_focused_row_when_nothing_is_selected`; `u41_shift_f2_does_nothing` and the other F2 tests keep passing as written.

**U141 the user's own append shows the new line.** Narrows U20. Found: after `append` on an Agent-owned Pad with a long text, the read-only text kept its scroll position, so the appended line sat out of sight. Given an open Agent-owned Pad, when the user's `append` call succeeds and the text refreshes, then the text scrolls to its end. A `pad.changed` from another actor refreshes the text and keeps the reader's scroll position (U20), and a failed `append` keeps the field's text and the scroll position. Tests (`u141_`): after the user's append the text is at its end; a `pad.changed` from an Agent keeps `scrollTop`; a failed append keeps `scrollTop`.
