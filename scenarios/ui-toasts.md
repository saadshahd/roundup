# UI: Toasts tell the user what needs them (U410 to U414)

Module: `apps/desktop` (the webview). Ids: U410 to U414, U3790 to U3793.

Found by the user's report #1111: roundup has no notification at all; the Dock badge (`rail/DockBadge.tsx`) only counts. The user asked for a clean toast and a short sound that hides by itself, with no bell icon, no badge in the window bar and no list of past toasts. GLOSSARY.md's _Signal_ lists "notification" as _Avoid_, so the implementing PR adds **Toast** to `GLOSSARY.md` (Contract recipe): "A small card with an Agent's Glyph, its name and one line, shown for a moment when that Agent needs the user, fails or changes the user's work; it hides by itself and is never kept in a list." Which Actor wrote a Todo or Pad is read from what the webview already receives: `todo.updated`, `pad.changed` and the Touch log (P2). If an event lacks the writer, the Builder adds it by the Contract recipe with every caller in the same PR. Playing the sound while roundup is not in front needs the App only if the webview cannot do it; then it is one App seam command named in `GLOSSARY.md`, with a fake in `src/testing/fakeApp.ts`. Observer: Vitest `u410_` to `u414_` against the fake App, a fake clock and the U26 harness at 1280 by 820 and 1280 by 800, light and dark (captures and critic Check readings use dark only, U3725), with `window.__checks()` in `artifacts/ux/U410/`. The sound's audibility and WKWebView's focus events stay the user's `just app` observation. Moves: D1, D2, D5, D6, D9.

**U410 which events make a Toast.** Given an open Project, when an Agent's Kind becomes `needs-you`, or becomes `error`, or an Agent other than the user changes a Todo whose `creator` is the user or a Pad the user owns, then one Toast shows and one sound plays. No Toast and no sound for: an Agent's Kind becoming `done`, `idle`, `working` or `blocked`; a Todo made by an Agent; a Pad an Agent owns; the user's own change; an Agent's Kind already `needs-you` on load; or a repeat of the same Kind. Tests: `u410_needs_you_makes_a_toast`, `u410_error_makes_a_toast`, `u410_an_agent_editing_the_users_todo_or_pad_makes_a_toast`, `u410_other_changes_make_none`, `u410_loading_a_project_makes_none`.

**U411 what a Toast shows and how it leaves.** Given a Toast, then it holds the Agent's `KindGlyph` (decorative), its name as the Rail shows it and one line of what happened (`Needs you`, `Failed`, `Changed Todo "<title>"`, `Changed Pad <name>`), cut with an ellipsis at one line; it is a `button` with an accessible name of the same words and sits in a polite live region. It hides by itself after 5 s on the fake clock; while the pointer is over it or focus is in it the timer stops, and leaving restarts the full 5 s. `Esc` with focus in it hides it. No bell, no counter in the window bar, no list and no history anywhere: after a Toast hides nothing of it is in the DOM. Tests: `u411_a_toast_shows_glyph_name_and_one_line`, `u411_it_hides_after_five_seconds`, `u411_hover_and_focus_keep_it`, `u411_nothing_of_it_stays`, `u411_no_bell_or_list_exists`.

**U412 clicking a Toast opens what it names.** _Amended only as listed by U3789e (`ui-shelf.md`); U3789a–d govern those replacement clauses._ Given a Toast for an Agent, when it is clicked, then that Agent's Rail row is selected as a click on the row does and the Toast hides; for a Todo or Pad, its Drawer opens as the Shelf's row does (U15) and the Toast hides. A Toast naming a removed Agent, Todo or Pad hides and shows U80's one failure line. Tests: `u412_clicking_selects_the_agent`, `u412_clicking_opens_the_todo_or_pad_drawer`, `u412_a_gone_target_shows_the_failure_line`.

**U413 when roundup is not in front, and for the selected Agent.** Given roundup is not the app in front (`document.hasFocus()` false or `visibilityState` hidden), when an event of U410 happens, then the sound plays at once and no Toast shows; when the user comes back, the Toasts that were held show, merged as U414 says, and no sound plays again. A Toast hidden by its timer while the window was away is not held. While roundup is in front, an event for the selected Agent makes no Toast and no sound; an event for another Agent does. Tests: `u413_away_plays_the_sound_and_holds_the_toast`, `u413_coming_back_shows_without_a_second_sound`, `u413_the_selected_agent_in_front_makes_none`.

**U414 few Toasts, never in the way, quiet when idle.** Given ten events inside 1 s, then at most 3 Toasts show at once, newest first, and the rest are one Toast `And N more`; one Agent's repeated events replace its Toast and play one sound per second at most. Its enter and exit are a 150 ms opacity and 8 px translate (`docs/motion.md`, `animate` skill), ease-out, and with `prefers-reduced-motion` they are instant. Colours and sizes are Tokens only. With no event, no timer, listener loop or audio object runs (rule 7): the layer renders nothing and a test counts zero pending timers. Tests: `u414_ten_events_make_at_most_three_toasts`, `u414_repeats_replace_and_one_sound_per_second`, `u414_motion_and_reduced_motion`, `u414_idle_runs_nothing`.

The owner's words (#3313): toasts should look and move like AffectUI's Notification Stack and Toaster, and no Toast stays for ever; each shows its time left as a bar, as Solo does. Notification Stack is MIT, so its CSS may be copied with credit; Toaster is paid, so it is rebuilt from its public docs (`https://www.affectui.com/llms-full.txt`) with no code copied. The existing Ark toast stays the base, colours and sizes are Tokens only, and U3790 to U3793 narrow the U411 timer and the U414 motion where they say so. Observer: Vitest `u3790_` to `u3793_` against the fake App, a fake clock and the U26 harness, dark only (U3725), with `window.__checks()` in `artifacts/ux/U3790/`; the look against AffectUI is the design critic's, not a test's. Moves: D2, D5, D6.

**U3790 every Toast has a time and shows it.** Given any Toast, including the failure Toast U412 and U503 show with U80's `✕` line, then a notice has a timer of 5 s on the fake clock and hides when the time is spent; the failure Toast has no timer (P3557c), shows no bar, and leaves with its item, a later save of it or its Close button. It shows the time left as a bar along its bottom edge, full when it appears and draining to empty at the same rate, whose remaining width is the time left over the whole time (a `progressbar` role is not used: the bar is decorative and the card's words already say it; `aria-hidden`). The bar is a Token colour, 2 px high, and adds no height to the card (#3294). No notice stays for ever: after 10 s of the fake clock with no pause (U3791) no notice is in the DOM. This narrows U411's 5 s and U80's "never clears on a timer" for a Toast only; U80's lines elsewhere keep their own rules. Tests: `u3790_a_notice_hides_at_five_seconds`, `u3790_a_failure_toast_has_no_time_and_closes_on_dismissal`, `u3790_the_bar_drains_with_the_time_left`, `u3790_no_toast_outlives_its_time`.

**U3791 the time pauses and resumes.** Given Toasts showing, when the pointer is over the stack or focus is in it, or `document.visibilityState` is `hidden`, then every bar and timer stops where it is; when all three are over, they resume from the time they had left, not from the full time. Three seconds of a 5 s notice, a hover and a leave leave it 2 s, not 5. The bar's drain runs only while the Toast is visible and the window is shown (#3298): while paused or hidden no animation frame, timer or CSS animation runs for it, and with no Toast none runs at all (U414's idle rule). This replaces U411's and U503's restart of the full 5 s after hover or focus. A Toast held while the window was away (U413) starts its time when it shows. Tests: `u3791_hover_pauses_and_leaving_resumes_from_what_was_left`, `u3791_focus_in_the_stack_pauses_it`, `u3791_a_hidden_window_pauses_it`, `u3791_a_paused_toast_runs_no_timer_or_animation`.

**U3792 the cards stack and fan out.** Given more than one Toast (at most three cards including the summary, U414), then they sit behind each other: the newest in front at full size, each one behind it smaller by 5% and 8 px higher, so its top edge peeks out above the one in front, and the cards behind do not take pointer or key focus. When the pointer is over the stack or focus is in it, the stack fans out into a list: the cards at full size with a gap of one Token spacing step, each one reachable by Tab, the order unchanged, and when the pointer leaves and focus is out it folds back. Esc, click and target navigation (U411, U412) act on the card as before and the stack closes the gap. Expiry or a dismissal of one card does not fold or unfold the rest. The stack sits in the layer order #3291 and #3295 fix, and beneath it the layout does not move (#3294). Tests: `u3792_cards_behind_are_smaller_and_peek_out`, `u3792_hover_and_focus_fan_the_stack_into_a_list`, `u3792_leaving_folds_it_back`, `u3792_only_the_front_card_is_focusable_when_folded`, `u3792_dismissing_a_card_closes_the_gap_without_folding`.

**U3793 how the cards move.** Given the stack, then a new Toast lifts in from the screen edge it sits at, 200 ms in `--easing-out`, while the cards behind move back one step; a dismissed or expired card fades and sinks 8 px in 150 ms while the cards behind it move up. Only transform and opacity change, never height or top. The bar's drain is the one motion with no act behind it (`docs/motion.md` gets rows for these, in the same PR). Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the cards switch at once with no transition and the bar still drains, since it is the only way to see the time left. Narrows U414's 150 ms opacity and 8 px translate for entering and leaving. Tests: `u3793_a_new_toast_lifts_in_and_the_rest_move_back`, `u3793_a_leaving_card_fades_and_sinks_while_the_rest_move_up`, `u3793_only_transform_and_opacity_animate`, `u3793_reduced_motion_switches_at_once_and_the_bar_still_drains`.
